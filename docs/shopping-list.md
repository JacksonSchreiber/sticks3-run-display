# Shopping list

Prices are what was listed when this was written; check before ordering.

## Electronics

- **M5Stack StickS3** (K150) — $21.50
- **Chest strap:** Coospo H9Z (USB rechargeable, ~50 h per charge) — $43.89 on sale, or Coospo H808S (CR2032 coin cell, ~300 h) — $35.09. Any strap using the standard Bluetooth heart rate service works.
- **USB-C data cable** — charge-only cables can't program the Stick

## 3D printing

The Adventurer 5M ships with a glue stick, diagonal pliers, hex keys and a 50 g PLA sample.

- **PLA filament, 1.75 mm, 1 kg** — mold and core
- **PETG filament, 1.75 mm, 1 kg** — clip mount
- **Digital calipers**
- **Isopropyl alcohol, 90%+** — cleaning the build plate
- **Sandpaper, 220-800 grit** — flattening the mold parting faces
- Optional: deburring tool, airtight bin + desiccant for filament

## Silicone casting

- **Smooth-On Smooth-Sil 945, 2 lb trial kit** (skin safe, Shore 45A, 1:1 by weight). Amazon alternative: SORTA-Clear 37, also skin safe, slightly softer, 4 h cure.
- **Mann Ease Release 200** — mold release spray
- **Silc Pig black pigment** (or any pigment made for platinum-cure silicone) — 1.5-2% by weight, never over 3%
- **Digital scale, 0.1 g**
- **Graduated mixing cups and wooden stir sticks**
- **Vinyl gloves** — latex stops platinum silicone from curing
- **60 ml catheter-tip syringes**, 2-3
- **3 mm leather hole punch** — strap holes
- **Hobby knife** — trimming the seam and vent nubs
- Safety glasses, mostly for the aerosol release spray

## Clip

- **theclip.com MCS-631SS** stainless spring clip — $4.25. Alternative: MCS-661, shorter but coated steel.

## Fasteners

One metric screw assortment covers every part: M2/M3, socket-cap or button head, with nuts and washers. Confirm the size chart lists **M2x6** and **M3x30**. Stainless holds up better than black alloy steel against sweat.

## Software (free)

- **Orca-Flashforge / Flash Studio** — slicer
- **OpenSCAD** — only to change a design
- **Arduino IDE 2** and **Android Studio** — later, for firmware and the app
